Anna

Anna is a feature-packed Discord bot built around a virtual economy, interactive games, rewards, collectibles, and social interactions.

About Anna

Build your balance, compete with friends, collect items, take risks, and climb the leaderboard!

Economy

Anna comes with a complete virtual currency system where users can earn, spend, save, and transfer their money.

  • Check your wallet and vault balance
  • Claim daily rewards with streaks
  • Send money to other users
  • Compete on the economy leaderboard
  • Deposit and withdraw money through your Vault
  • Ask other users for money with the Begging system

Games & Gambling

Put your balance on the line with several interactive games:

  • Coin Flip — Bet and try your luck
  • Number Guess — Open mystery boxes and cash out before hitting a losing box
  • Car Race — Challenge other users in a multiplayer race
  • Rock Paper Scissors — Multiplayer matches with real bets
  • Trivia — 7 rounds with increasing rewards and optional cashouts
  • Word Scramble — Solve increasingly difficult scrambled words
  • TTS Crossword — Solve English clues and risk your rewards for higher payouts

Each game has its own cooldowns and reward mechanics.

Gacha & Inventory

Spend your currency on gacha packs and collect different items.

Possible rewards include:

  • Thief items
  • Guard items
  • Vaults
  • Collectible dolls
  • Collectible pets
  • Zonk rewards

Collect items in your inventory, use special items, and sell your collectible dolls and pets for currency.

Thief & Guard System

Anna adds a competitive economy mechanic with Thief and Guard items.

  • Thief Tier A — 10%
  • Thief Tier B — 20%
  • Thief Tier C — 30%
  • Guard Tier A — 10%
  • Guard Tier B — 20%
  • Guard Tier C — 30%

Use your items strategically and protect your balance from other players.

Vault System

Store your money safely with Vault items.

Different Vault tiers provide different storage capacities and durations, including a permanent Tier C Vault with 100,000 capacity.

Anna Pass

Anna also features an optional 30-day Anna Pass.

  • 30-day access
  • 10,000 Anna per day
  • Up to 300,000 Anna over 30 days

Anna Top-Up & Premium Items

Users can request Anna currency top-ups and premium items directly through the bot.

Anna provides multiple top-up packages and premium items such as:

  • Thief Tier C
  • Guard Tier C
  • Vault Tier C
  • Anna Pass

Purchase requests are sent directly to the bot owner through Discord so they can be handled manually.

Persistent Economy

Anna uses a persistent database to keep user balances, transactions, inventory, rewards, passes, and cooldown-related data across bot restarts.

Time-based systems and cooldowns are designed to continue from their saved timestamps instead of simply resetting whenever Anna restarts.

Useful Commands

  • anna cash — Check your balance
  • anna daily — Claim your daily reward
  • anna give @user <amount> — Transfer money
  • anna leaderboard — View the richest users
  • anna shop — View gacha packs
  • anna gacha <1-3> — Open a gacha pack
  • anna inv — View your inventory
  • anna deposit <amount> — Deposit into your Vault
  • anna withdraw <amount> — Withdraw from your Vault
  • anna thief @user [tier] — Attempt to steal money
  • anna tv <bet> — Play Trivia
  • anna sb <bet> — Play Word Scramble
  • anna tts <bet> — Play the crossword-style puzzle
  • anna cf <bet> — Play Coin Flip
  • anna ta <bet> — Play Number Guess
  • anna rc <bet> — Start a Car Race
  • anna st @users — Play Rock Paper Scissors
  • anna begging <amount> @user — Ask another user for money

Use anna help in your server to see the available commands.
